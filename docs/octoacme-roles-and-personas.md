# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### How they interact with other roles
- **With Product Managers**: Receive requirements and acceptance criteria; provide feasibility estimates
- **With Project Managers**: Update task status and escalate blockers
- **With Quality Assurance Leads**: Collaborate on test coverage and acceptance criteria validation
- **With Technical Leads / Architects**: Follow architectural guidance and design decisions

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### How they interact with other roles
- **With Developers**: Define requirements, review solutions, accept completed work
- **With Project Managers**: Align on priorities and milestone goals
- **With Business Analysts**: Refine user stories and requirements
- **With Stakeholders / Sponsors**: Manage expectations and gather business context

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### How they interact with other roles
- **With all team members**: Facilitate daily standups, planning, and retrospectives
- **With Product Managers**: Align on priorities and schedule delivery milestones
- **With Scrum Masters / Agile Coaches**: Coordinate on process improvements and removal of blockers
- **With Stakeholders / Sponsors**: Provide status updates and escalate risks

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance Leads own the testing strategy, quality standards, and acceptance criteria validation. They work closely with product and development teams to ensure features meet quality gates before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define and maintain quality standards and acceptance criteria
- Create and execute test plans (unit, integration, end-to-end, performance)
- Identify and track defects, prioritize by impact
- Collaborate with developers on testability and test coverage
- Perform user acceptance testing (UAT) coordination
- Validate release readiness against quality gates

### Goals
- Ensure high-quality, reliable features reaching production
- Reduce post-release defects and incidents
- Establish consistent quality processes across projects

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- Test plan reviews with developers and product managers
- Bug reports and quality metrics dashboards
- Release readiness gates and sign-offs

### How they interact with other roles
- **With Developers**: Collaborate on test coverage, design for testability, and defect resolution
- **With Product Managers**: Validate acceptance criteria and coordinate UAT
- **With Technical Leads / Architects**: Ensure quality standards align with technical architecture
- **With Project Managers**: Report quality metrics and validate release readiness

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide architectural guidance, design decisions, and technical risk mitigation. They work across the delivery team to ensure technical solutions align with business goals and system reliability.

### Responsibilities
- Guide technical design and architecture decisions
- Identify and mitigate technical risks and dependencies
- Review code and design proposals for scalability and maintainability
- Mentor developers and support capability building
- Coordinate with external systems and dependencies
- Ensure alignment with technical standards and best practices

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable, and reliable solutions
- Reduce technical debt and production incidents
- Support team growth and technical excellence

### Typical Communication
- Design review sessions and architecture discussions
- Code review comments and technical guidance
- Risk registers and dependency tracking
- Technical debt backlog items

### How they interact with other roles
- **With Developers**: Provide architectural guidance, mentor on design patterns, and review code
- **With Product Managers**: Discuss technical trade-offs and feasibility of requirements
- **With Quality Assurance Leads**: Ensure architecture supports testability and performance goals
- **With Project Managers**: Identify and escalate technical risks and dependencies

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters / Agile Coaches facilitate Agile ceremonies, remove blockers, and coach the team on Agile practices. They serve the team and organization to adopt and improve iterative delivery processes.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate daily standups, sprint planning, reviews, and retrospectives
- Remove blockers and impediments that hinder team progress
- Coach team members on Agile principles and practices
- Track and report on sprint metrics and team velocity
- Identify process improvements and drive adoption
- Protect the team from external distractions during sprints

### Goals
- Enable the team to deliver consistently and sustainably
- Foster a culture of continuous improvement
- Improve team autonomy and self-organization

### Typical Communication
- Sprint ceremonies (standups, planning, reviews, retrospectives)
- One-on-one coaching sessions with team members
- Process improvement discussions and action item tracking
- Impediment logs and process metrics

### How they interact with other roles
- **With Developers**: Remove blockers, facilitate collaboration, coach on estimation
- **With Product Managers**: Ensure product backlog is refined and ready for sprint planning
- **With Project Managers**: Collaborate on milestone tracking and escalation paths
- **With all team members**: Create psychological safety and encourage feedback

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders / Sponsors provide business context, strategic direction, and approvals for projects. They ensure the project aligns with organizational goals and secure necessary resources and budget.

### Responsibilities
- Provide business context and strategic priorities
- Approve project charter and major milestones
- Allocate budget and resources for the project
- Review and validate progress against business objectives
- Serve as escalation point for critical decisions
- Support team and communicate project value to the organization

### Goals
- Ensure project delivery aligns with organizational strategy
- Maximize return on investment and business value
- Maintain stakeholder confidence and executive visibility

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder updates and milestone reviews
- Budget and resource allocation discussions
- Decision approvals and milestone sign-offs
- Executive briefings and status reports

### How they interact with other roles
- **With Product Managers**: Align on business objectives and measure success
- **With Project Managers**: Review status, approve changes, escalate risks
- **With Developers and delivery team**: Communicate strategic importance and business context
- **With other Stakeholders**: Coordinate organizational alignment and dependencies

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts bridge the gap between business needs and technical implementation. They gather requirements, document user stories, and validate solutions against business needs to ensure delivered features provide real value.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and document business requirements from stakeholders
- Create and refine user stories with clear acceptance criteria
- Conduct stakeholder interviews and workshops
- Validate that delivered solutions meet business requirements
- Identify and document business processes and workflows
- Support requirement traceability and change management

### Goals
- Ensure delivered features meet business needs and provide measurable value
- Reduce rework caused by unclear or misaligned requirements
- Improve communication between business and technical teams

### Typical Communication
- Requirement workshops and stakeholder interviews
- User story documentation and refinement
- Requirements traceability matrices and acceptance testing
- Stakeholder validation and sign-offs

### How they interact with other roles
- **With Product Managers**: Refine requirements and validate business value
- **With Developers**: Clarify requirements and discuss implementation feasibility
- **With Quality Assurance Leads**: Define acceptance criteria and coordinate UAT
- **With Stakeholders / Sponsors**: Gather business requirements and validate solutions
- **With Project Managers**: Document scope and identify requirement changes
